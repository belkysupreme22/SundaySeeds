# Study badge artwork

Five transparent medal assets for the local study badge collection. The
artwork draws on the faceted achievement medals in
[reference 10](../../../../docs/references/reference-10.png).

The approved SundaySeeds concept sheet was generated with the built-in image
generation tool. The export prompt asked for the original shapes, enamel
colors, raised symbols and ribbons isolated on a transparent three-column
sheet without labels or a background. The five used medals were cropped to
their alpha bounds with a transparent margin and sized to 288 px on their
longest edge. The course flag remains a concept for connected course awards.

## Export prompt

> Production asset export, precise object edit. Extract the six approved
> SundaySeeds medal artworks as transparent cutouts into a regular 3-column by
> 2-row sprite sheet, identical order and art as input. Remove ALL text, cream
> page and grid lines. Truly transparent canvas between all objects and around
> the edge, including spaces between ribbon tails. Do not redraw, reinterpret,
> or change the shape, colors, central raised artwork, weave, or ribbons of any
> medal. Each entire isolated medal centered within its equal tile, similar
> visual dimensions, about 78% of tile height, all points and ribbon ends
> inside its own tile with generous transparent gutters, no contact between
> cells. Outer shadows clipped before neighboring cells. Row 1: forest
> calendar with two ticks, warm peach flame, wheat rising sun. Row 2: rose
> diamond rising chevrons with star, green book and return arrow on bookmark,
> forest and wheat course flag. The target is production-ready isolated
> transparent art assets, no headings, labels, border, backdrop, watermarks or
> added content. Aspect ratio 3:2 with equal square cells.
