# Single mobile course library

Accepted by the owner on 6 November 2026.

Courses is the single place to discover learner content. Guests can browse
published offerings and course outlines. Joining a course requires a verified
account. Lessons, reading places, and knowledge checks follow the learner's
joined edition. The Home card previews the same catalog and shows a local
placeholder while it loads.

Old standalone lesson, reader, and quiz links open Courses. The old Study
Badges link opens Progress. The mobile app no longer reads or writes the
standalone study snapshot. Its device records remain available for a future
migration decision. Connected course progress begins with recorded activity in
the joined edition.

The Progress tab directs learners to progress inside their joined courses.
The public catalog describes current offerings. A connected Study Shelf and
account-wide badges need an authorized read of the learner's own enrollments
and confirmed study events, including any pinned editions no longer listed.
The [Study Shelf plan](../implementation-plan.md) records those requirements.
