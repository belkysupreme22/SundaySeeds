# Study activity badges

Accepted for the first mobile increment on 5 November 2026.

Progress places Weekly Rhythm below the lesson resume card. A badge collection
shows weekly and daily streak levels, cumulative study days, distinct lesson
revisits and the latest personal best. The approved reference 10 direction uses
raised medal artwork, lock markers and countable diamond stitches.

One finished lesson quiz records a local calendar day. Retakes also qualify.
Weekly Rhythm counts two distinct days in a Monday-to-Sunday week, with earned
levels at one, three and six consecutive qualifying weeks. The best daily
streak earns levels at three, seven and fourteen days. Cumulative study days
earn levels at three, seven, fourteen and thirty days. Revisits count each
distinct lesson once after a quiz is finished on a later recorded day. Its
five-lesson tier is shown when reachable. Personal Best compares scores within
the same lesson and requires a strict improvement. A break resets progress
toward the next consecutive streak tier while preserving previously earned
levels.

Activity is saved in the existing device-local learning snapshot alongside quiz
progress and resets with it. Older scores provide a comparison baseline. Past
dates are unknown. Connected course medals require confirmed course and study
history across the learner's account and are outside this increment.

Recent activity on Progress shows the latest six recorded local quiz finishes,
including retakes and personal bests. It uses the recorded study date and
attempt score, and older snapshots start with an empty timeline. The weekly
goal and the learner's actual number of study days are labelled separately.

The owner requested smaller badge cards and removal of the separate lesson
milestones board. Progress presents the lesson summary and resume action,
Weekly Rhythm, and Recent activity. The badge collection uses compact paired
cards with a single-column fallback for larger text.

Source, locale and art export checks were performed. Device review and app
verification are pending. See the [learner audit](../learner-screen-audit.md).
