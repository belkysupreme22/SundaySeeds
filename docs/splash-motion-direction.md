# SundaySeeds continuous book-to-tree motion

Updated 3 November 2026 after the owner rejected the layered-image growth attempt.
This is an animation production brief, not a completed or approved film.

## Confirmed direction

Naturalistic 3D commercial motion. One closed book appears, opens at its hinge,
then several flexible sheets turn as if finding a page. They settle to reveal a
seed inside the page crease. The seed expands and germinates, a small tree grows
from that exact point, branches extend, leaves unfurl and fruit develops last.
The app icon remains a separate, static graphic with a readable connected
book/trunk/canopy silhouette.

The rejected approach used separate generated book, wood, canopy and fruit PNGs
with clipping, scaling and opacity. Those files and their component are archived
under the ignored `.artifacts/artwork-refresh-2026-11-03/superseded-layered-splash/`.
They are no longer imported by startup. A short logo transition remains while the
replacement is produced; it does not claim to depict the requested growth.

## One continuous shot

The eight-second timing below is a working motion target for review, not an
approved mandatory wait on every app launch.

| Time | Action | Required continuity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–0.6s | A closed forest cloth-bound book settles into view | Same physical book throughout; paper edges and cover thickness visible |
| 0.6–1.5s | The front cover rotates open around its spine | Cover stays connected; real perspective and contact shadows |
| 1.2–2.6s | Several thin paper sheets turn with overlapping timing | Sheets bend and curl from the binding; no rigid-card flipping |
| 2.6–3.2s | Pages settle; the seed rests in the inner crease | Seed is revealed by the moving paper, not faded onto the scene |
| 3.2–4.3s | Seed swells, splits and sends out a root and shoot | Shoot visibly originates inside the seed; roots stay in the crease |
| 4.0–5.8s | Trunk lengthens and thickens; branches extend | Growth travels along curved stems; no whole-tree scaling or wipe reveal |
| 4.8–6.7s | Leaf buds unfold individually along branches | Each leaf stays attached and opens with slightly different timing |
| 6.4–7.5s | Small fruit buds swell into mature golden fruit | Fruit stays on stalks; no sudden popping or floating ornaments |
| 7.5–8.0s | The living tree settles with a restrained leaf sway | Hold the complete book/tree silhouette before the app transition |

Use a softly lit, pale paper studio environment close to `#FCFAFF`, a forest-green
cloth book, warm ivory paper, believable bark, natural green leaves and modest
golden fruit. Keep the whole book and eventual crown in frame. An elevated
three-quarter, long-lens view should show the page action without hiding the seed
behind the binding. Leave clear space for a separately rendered app wordmark.
No hands, religious ornament, generated writing, sparkles, confetti, cuts,
dissolves between growth states or unrelated camera moves.

## Production routes and current state

Runway was discovered through the plugin catalogue and suggested for genuine
video generation. Its tool definitions subsequently became visible, but two
`whoami` calls returned `Unknown tool (runway.whoami)`. Authentication, account
availability and generation remain unconfirmed. No generation was submitted and
no credit use is claimed. Do not describe a visible tool definition as a working
connection. Recheck authentication before selecting an available model.

The deterministic alternative is an editable procedural Blender scene in
`apps/mobile/scripts/splash-motion/`. A modeled scene permits exact cover hinges,
paper bending and growing branches instead of relying on a video model to invent
all these events correctly. Script authoring does not establish render quality:
Blender execution, actual renders, motion review and material refinement remain
separate work. No renderer is currently installed in the checked executable paths.

## Generation prompt

> One uninterrupted naturalistic 3D product animation in a soft warm-white studio.
> A closed dark forest-green cloth-bound book is centered low in frame, with room
> above for a tree. Its front cover smoothly opens on the binding. Several thin
> ivory paper pages lift, bend and flutter across the spine in overlapping turns,
> like finding a particular page. The pages settle, revealing a small brown seed
> nestled inside the inner page crease. The seed swells gradually, cracks into two
> attached husk halves, sends tiny roots into the crease and a green shoot upward.
> In continuous organic growth, the shoot lengthens into a slender woody trunk;
> curved branches extend from its tips. Small leaf buds open individually into
> natural green leaves, building a graceful small tree. Only after the branches
> and foliage grow, tiny fruit buds on attached stalks slowly swell into several
> golden fruits. The same book, seed and tree remain spatially consistent for the
> entire take. Physically plausible cloth, paper, bark and leaf materials; gentle
> studio lighting and contact shadows. Fixed elevated three-quarter camera showing
> the hinge, bending pages and seed origin; entire book and crown stay visible.
> End with the completed living fruit tree growing from inside the open book.
> Silent, no text, no cuts, no crossfades, no slideshow, no image-layer reveals,
> no instant whole-tree appearance, no detached fruit, no hands or sparkles.

  A storyboard or single final frame cannot verify page bending or organic growth.
- Preserve the native static launch screen. The film belongs in the subsequent app
  layer, with local playback, no network dependency and no generated text.
- Start from a square composition, target 30fps and H.264 MP4 with no audio track.
  Verify the actual output codec, duration, dimensions and file size; do not assume
  the generator follows export requests. Keep a lossless final-frame still.
- Decide playback tooling only after a usable film exists. No video dependency is
  added yet. Preserve font/layout startup gating, routing, touch/keyboard isolation,
  a finite load/playback timeout and static fallback.
- Reduced motion uses the static mark or approved final frame. A late preference
  change stops playback. Cleanup must release the player; background/foreground
  behavior needs device review. Do not replay an eight-second brand film on every
  navigation or describe it as evidence of app loading progress.
- Owner review remains required for material realism, seed-to-trunk continuity,
  page/branch intersections, timing, small screens and the startup experience.
  Tests, typecheck, builds and app/native runtime checks have not run for this work.
